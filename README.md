# Mine Hunter

Free single-file HTML5 minesweeper. Hunt mines on a neon checkerboard across
three fields — Rookie 9×9, Scout 12×12, Veteran 16×16. First click is always
safe, floods open in a glow wave, and your best times are saved per difficulty.
One HTML file, zero assets.

**Play:** https://shuangyueliao.github.io/mine-hunter/

- Tap / click to dig, long-press or right-click to flag, DIG/FLAG mode toggle
- Double-click / double-tap a number to chord-open its neighbors
- Safe first click (mines placed after it), BFS flood reveal with pop animation
- Mine counter, timer, per-difficulty best times (localStorage), mute toggle
- Procedural neon visuals and WebAudio sounds, no dependencies, no tracking
