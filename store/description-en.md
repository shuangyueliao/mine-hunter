# Mine Hunter — Store Submission Materials

## Short description (CrazyGames / GameDistribution / itch.io)

Neon minesweeper with a guaranteed-safe first click. Clear Rookie, Scout and
Veteran fields, flag what you fear, and race your best time. How fast can you
clear the field?

(171 characters)

## Details

- Genre: Puzzle / Casual / Logic
- Controls: Mouse / Touch — tap or click to dig, long-press or right-click
  to flag, DIG/FLAG mode toggle for touch, double-click or double-tap a
  number to chord-open its neighbors.
- Features: 3 difficulties (Rookie 9x9/10 mines, Scout 12x12/24 mines,
  Veteran 16x16/40 mines), first click + its neighborhood always mine-free
  (mines placed after the first click), BFS flood reveal with pop animation,
  mine counter, timer, per-difficulty best times saved locally, lose board
  reveals all mines and marks wrong flags, mute toggle, neon checkerboard
  visuals with glowing mines, procedural graphics & sounds, single-file
  HTML5 (no external assets), works offline once loaded.
- Tech: Vanilla JavaScript, Canvas, WebAudio. No frameworks, no tracking.
